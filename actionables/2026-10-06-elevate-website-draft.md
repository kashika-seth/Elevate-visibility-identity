## Decisions taken

1. **Positioning:** Elevate is a **full-range service provider to universities and colleges**, not an operator of campuses or brands. "**Full stack higher education**" was the preferred phrase.
2. **Regulatory test:** Every line on the public site has to be regulator-proof and non-consumer-facing. The site is more public than any investor or partner deck, so anything we'd strip from a deck for a wider audience comes off the site as well.
3. **Go-live:** The site launches once the positioning and copy changes are done. It doesn't wait for the full team-section decision, and other sections get added after launch.
4. **Awards:** All awards (Sunstone, Mirai and the other brands) are shown under Elevate.
5. **Team section (for now):** Only the three founders, Neeraj, Chirag and Sakshi, with designations. These are pure-play Elevate roles. Brand-facing people come later.
6. **Email IDs:** Elevate-function people move to Elevate email IDs, and anyone can add Elevate on LinkedIn under their current designation.
